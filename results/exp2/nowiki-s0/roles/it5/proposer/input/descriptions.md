pick_and_place: ALWAYS load for a plain 'put a X in/on Y' or 'put some X on Y' task with ONE object and no clean/hot/cool/heat wording, no lamp, no 'two'. Find, take, and place it.
pick_clean_then_place: ALWAYS load when the task says 'clean' (e.g. 'put a clean plate in the cabinet', 'clean X and put it in Y'). Take the object, clean it at the sinkbasin, then place it.
pick_cool_then_place: ALWAYS load when the task says 'cool', 'cold', 'chilled' (e.g. 'put a cool apple on the table', 'cool X and put it in Y'). Take the object, cool it in the fridge, then place it.
pick_heat_then_place: ALWAYS load when the task says 'hot', 'heat', 'warm', 'cook' (e.g. 'put a hot potato on the countertop', 'heat X and put it in Y'). Take the object, heat it in the microwave, then place it.
pick_two_obj_and_place: ALWAYS load when the task contains 'two' of an object (e.g. 'put two pillows on the sofa', 'find two cds and put them in the drawer'). Find and place two objects of the same kind, one at a time.
